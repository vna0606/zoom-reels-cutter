# ITBooster design system capture

## Overview

ITBooster uses a dark, restrained developer-platform aesthetic with a centered hero, modular card grids and small terminal-like labels. The page is primarily charcoal and soft white, with mint used for actions, status and thin emphasis. Cards are softly rounded, nearly flat and separated by quiet borders plus a subtle inset glow. Product imagery mixes monochrome technical forms with occasional mint-lit 3D objects.

## Colors

- **Primary surface**: `#191919` — page and video canvas.
- **Raised surface**: `#2F2F2F` — cards and secondary panels.
- **Primary content**: `#F6F6F6` — headings and key labels.
- **Muted content**: `#747474` — secondary copy and metadata.
- **Mint accent**: `#AEFBC3` — actions, active state, underlines and focus.
- **Deep black**: `#0E0E11` — high-contrast inset areas.
- **Rare utility purple**: `#7A52FF` — announcement-only micro-accent.

## Typography

- **Primary**: Onest Variable, weights 400, 500 and 700; source range 100–900.
- **Display**: Onest 700, compact line height and neutral tracking.
- **Section heading**: Onest 500.
- **Body**: Onest 400.
- **Code labels**: system monospace for short functional markers only.

## Elevation

Depth comes from `1px` low-opacity light borders, `14–16px` corner radii and a quiet `0 0 24px` inset glow. Mint focus states may use an inset glow derived from `rgba(174,251,195,.30)`. Avoid glossy glass, strong external shadows and full-screen gradients.

## Components

- **Terminal status chip** — mint text inside a dark pill with a soft mint inset glow.
- **Feature card matrix** — asymmetrical two- and three-column dark cards with a technical object anchored to a corner.
- **Mint action pill** — solid mint, dark label and fully rounded ends.
- **Code underline** — monospace attribution with a thin mint underline.
- **Quiet technical panel** — rounded dark surface, light/5 border and restrained inset glow.
- **Orbital technical glyph** — grayscale line or mesh object rotating slowly inside a small dark tile.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use exact charcoal, soft-white and mint tokens from the capture.
- Keep cards dark and quiet; reserve mint for focus and state.
- Use Onest at video scale with 14–16px radii and visible 2px structural borders.
- Use functional code labels and blinking cursor motifs sparingly.
- Alternate dense card structures with strong typographic frames.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn every surface mint or purple.
- Do not use the ITBooster logo or claim affiliation; this is a style preset.
- Do not use generic blue/purple gradients or neon cyberpunk effects.
- Do not use sharp rectangular cards or heavy blurred drop shadows.
- Do not shrink the website's UI sizes directly into a video frame.
